## Context

What prompted this. The customer, the promise, the history. Say what the product does today and what is wrong or missing. Link the earlier decisions this one builds on or amends.

## Decision

What we decided, in the imperative: *we send one reminder, 24 hours before, and never between 9pm and 8am*. Every judgement that a person has to own goes here in plain words: the defaults, the limits, the exceptions.

## Considered and turned down

Each alternative in one or two sentences, and why not. This is the part an agent cannot recover from the code.

## Consequences

What this decision means going forward: what gets simpler, what gets harder, what follow-up it creates, and which documents it makes out of date.
